Gradient descent

Use when: No closed form, or "do two iterations".
Formula / fact
Conditions needed
Trap
w⁽ᵏ⁾ = w⁽ᵏ⁻¹⁾ − η ∇f(w⁽ᵏ⁻¹⁾)
Needsη>0 learning rate, f differentiable.
TrapUpdate every coordinate with the OLD point, simultaneously.
∇f = [∂f/∂x₁, ..., ∂f/∂xn]ᵀ points to steepest ascent
TrapDescent uses the negative gradient. Gradient is perpendicular to level sets.
η too small: slow. Moderately large: bounces and plateaus. Too large: diverges
Stop when ‖∇f‖ ≤ ε, or f decrease ≤ ε, or ‖w⁽ᵏ⁾−w⁽ᵏ⁻¹⁾‖ ≤ ε, or k = max

Direction-by-direction search needs 2ⁿ checks
TrapDoes not scale. That is why the gradient is used.

Needsdy/dw₁ = w₂ φ′(θ₁) x
TrapBackpropagation is repeated chain rule.
